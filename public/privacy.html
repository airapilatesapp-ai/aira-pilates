Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains what Aira collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Your e-mail address, to create your account and send you sign-in links.
  • Your subscription status (plan, trial and renewal dates), which Stripe shares with us so we know you have access.
  • Your progress in the app: the first name you choose to show, your goals, level, favourite classes, language and the days and minutes you practiced. This lets your progress follow you to a new phone.
  • Quiz answers on our website, saved only in your own browser.

We do not collect your card details (Stripe handles them), your location, contacts, photos or health records. Voice guidance is generated by your own device and no audio is recorded.

Who helps us run Aira

  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Supabase stores your account and progress.
  • Resend delivers our e-mails.
  • Vercel hosts the website and app.

They only use your data to provide their service to us. We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

Advertising

If we use ad pixels on our website (for example from Meta or TikTok) to measure our ads, we will update this policy and show a notice first. The app itself has no ads.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your progress and e-mail within 30 days, except billing records we must keep by law.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to airapilates.app@gmail.com. If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you have these rights too, and we will not treat you differently for using them.

Children

Aira is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you by e-mail or in the app.
