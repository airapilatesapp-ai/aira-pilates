Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains what Aira collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Your e-mail address, to create your account and send you sign-in links.
  • Your subscription status (plan, trial and renewal dates), which Stripe shares with us so we know you have access.
  • Your progress in the app: the first name you choose to show, your goals, level, favourite classes, language and the days and minutes you practiced. This lets your progress follow you to a new phone.
  • Quiz answers. If you take our quiz and give us your e-mail, we keep your e-mail, the first name you type (optional), your answers (goal, age range, focus areas, how you want to feel, level, days and minutes, what has held you back) and the classes we suggested. We use them to send you your plan and up to three follow-up e-mails, and to improve the quiz. Your answers about discomfort or pain stay in your own browser: we only use them to pick gentler classes and never send them to us.

We do not collect your card details (Stripe handles them), your location, contacts, photos or health records. Voice guidance is generated by your own device and no audio is recorded.

E-mails

If you give us your e-mail in the quiz, we send you your plan right away and at most three e-mails in total about it. We stop as soon as you start your free trial, or when you click "Unsubscribe" at the bottom of any of them. Members also get e-mails needed for their account, like sign-in codes and receipts.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits and how many people reach each step of the quiz. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally.

Who helps us run Aira

  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Supabase stores your account and progress.
  • Resend delivers our e-mails.
  • Vercel hosts the website and app.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) helps us measure our ads, as explained below.

Stripe, Supabase, Resend and Vercel only use your data to provide their service to us. We do not sell your personal information for money.

Advertising

We advertise on Instagram and Facebook. To learn which ads bring people who like Aira, our website pages (home, quiz and welcome page) use the Meta Pixel, and our server sends the same signals to Meta through its Conversions API. Meta places cookies for this.

What Meta receives: the pages you visit on our website, and when you leave your e-mail in the quiz, start checkout, start your free trial or pay. With these, Meta gets your e-mail in scrambled (hashed) form, your IP address and browser type, and Meta's own cookie IDs. Meta never receives your quiz answers, and the pixel's automatic scanning of buttons and pages is turned off. The app itself has no pixel and no ads.

Under some US state privacy laws, this counts as "sharing" personal information for advertising. You can opt out at any time:

Done. This browser won't send ad signals to Meta anymore.

We also respect the Global Privacy Control signal: if your browser sends it, we don't load the Meta Pixel. You can manage how Meta uses your data in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your progress and e-mail within 30 days, except billing records we must keep by law.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to airapilates.app@gmail.com. If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you have these rights too, and we will not treat you differently for using them.

Children

Aira is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you by e-mail or in the app.
