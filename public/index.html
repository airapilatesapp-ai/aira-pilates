Questions

Good to know

Do I need any equipment?

Just a mat or a soft rug. Every class is done on the floor or standing, using your own body weight.

I've never done Pilates. Is it for me?

Yes. Pick Beginner and Aira gives you simpler versions of each move, longer breaks and a 14-day path to learn the basics.

How does the free trial work?

You enter your card to start, but you're not charged for 7 days. Cancel before then in two taps and you pay nothing.

How do I cancel?

Open Aira, go to Progress and tap Manage subscription. You can cancel, switch plans or update your card yourself, any time.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, Android, tablets and computers. Aira opens in your browser and you can add it to your home screen like any app. No app store download needed.

Is it safe if I'm pregnant or have an injury?

Please talk to your doctor before starting any exercise program, especially during pregnancy, after surgery or with back pain. Stop any move that hurts.
